Edges by their two sides, 2026-11-15 (ray tracer, transport; unified teapot, 960x600; a loaded machine)

An edge pixel was finished by four more samples, each solved from the pixel's predictions.
Now (RenderOptions::edge_mode 2) it is a mix of its own colour and its neighbour's across the edge,
by how much of the pixel the nearer surface covers. Where that surface ends between the two pixel
centres is found by two rays tried against it from the nearer pixel's own hit (to an eighth of a pixel).

Against a reference of nine samples a pixel (2880x1800 averaged), rms error in 8-bit units over the
pixels the four samples change:

  close             16.4         9.9          11.0
  from above        17.9        12.5          11.3
  1976 version      14.2         9.2          10.7
On the further pixels only the new way changes: 11.7 -> 6.0 (default), 16.0 -> 5.0 (from above),
8.8 -> 8.4 (close), 7.4 -> 7.8 (1976).

Surface evaluations a frame, default view: 179 398 with no edges, 366 323 with four samples,
242 864 with two sides. Frame on one thread: 151 to 189 ms -> 115 to 122 ms (default view),
354 to 393 -> 266 to 318 ms (chrome on a polished floor), unchanged in a close view.

Tried first and dropped: placing the edge with no rays, from the cosine at the hit and at the pixel
one further in (c^2 = 2 k s) and from the hit's distance to the end of its sheet. It fails where the
surface's curvature changes within a pixel (rims, lips): rms 11.6 where four samples give 9.5.

Prediction, 2026-11-15: lattice pieces of a closed solid that show their inward side are left out

Every lattice cell was drawn into the pixels' candidate lists as two triangles, the far side of the
teapot and the inner wall behind the outer one included. A piece of a closed solid that is turned
so as to show the side inside the material cannot be seen, and now predicts nothing. Which side
that is, is found once per set of parts by trial rays (find_inward); for predictions the side whose
rays got out less than half as often as the other's is taken as inside.
