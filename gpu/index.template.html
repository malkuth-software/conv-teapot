The Utah Teapot in CONV geometry, on the GPU

The same teapot as the CPU version, drawn by WebGL 2. The CONV surface is evaluated in the shaders, at every vertex and at every pixel, from the same lattices; the line under the picture says how far the shaders' surface is from the CPU's.

GPU

The engine (WebAssembly) builds the lattices, lays out a mesh in (u, v), runs the rigid body and answers the pointer. Everything drawn is computed on the GPU: the CONV surface at each vertex and, with the box above ticked, again at each pixel for its normal; the shadow map; the trims of the joins; the cutaway's cut face; and reflections from emitters.

Simulation

Drag the teapot: it is held at the point you picked. Drag the background, or hold Shift, to orbit. Scroll to zoom. Double-click to poke. Arrow keys fly the camera, Page Up and Page Down raise and lower it, Shift slows it, Home returns it.
